The Challenge

Tenacore operates in a regulated, detail-driven industry where every claim matters and every visual has to communicate trust. Their marketing materials had grown inconsistent over time — different vendors, different formats, no unified look — and the company was heading into a key trade show season.

The work needed to meet a high bar for accuracy and compliance while still being visually compelling. Technical audiences don't want spin — they want clarity, and they can tell the difference.

The Approach

I worked with Tenacore's marketing and product teams to create a cohesive suite of materials across advertising, trade show graphics, and print collateral. The system prioritized clear product hierarchy, consistent brand voice, and production quality that held up at large format.

Trade show graphics were designed for impact at distance and legibility up close — a balance that requires tight control over scale, contrast, and message density. Print collateral was structured to support the sales process, with spec sheets, product comparisons, and leave-behinds all built on a shared template system.

Production

Trade Show & Collateral System

Coordinated suite of print-ready materials — from large-format trade show panels to tabloid sell sheets — built on consistent brand standards.

The Result

A cohesive marketing system that let Tenacore's products speak clearly in any format or setting.
