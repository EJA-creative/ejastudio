Why It's Here

Makita isn't a branding project. It's here because it shows what working inside a major consumer brand actually looks like — high standards, real deadlines, and the discipline to execute cleanly within a system someone else built. That's a skill set just as relevant as building one from scratch.

The Work

I came in as an intern inside Makita USA's corporate marketing department — and performed well enough to be brought back on contract. The anchor deliverable was the annual product catalog: a large-format publication covering Makita's full tool lineup. I handled production layout and contributed product photography — shooting tools on white background, editing for consistency, and ingesting assets into the internal database for use across print and digital channels.
