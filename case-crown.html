The Challenge

Crown Polymers makes high-performance specialty coatings for demanding applications — but their web presence didn't reflect the quality of their products. An outdated site with inconsistent branding was losing them credibility at the first point of contact for potential customers and distributors.

The challenge wasn't just a redesign — it was aligning a new digital presence with a brand that had evolved over time without formal standards. Every design decision had to bridge where they were and where they were headed.

The Approach

I led the creative direction for the site while working directly with their development partner to ensure what was designed could be built without compromise. The process started with brand alignment — establishing a consistent visual language — before a single page was laid out.

The final site organized their product catalog clearly, communicated their technical depth without overwhelming visitors, and gave their sales team a tool they could actually use to support conversations with prospects.

Design

Site Architecture & Visual Direction

Homepage layout and product section wireframes — translated from brand direction into a buildable design system.

The Result

A web presence that finally matched the quality of the products Crown Polymers has spent decades building.
