Creative Direction / Web / Project Management

Crown Polymers
Website Overhaul

Client

Crown Polymers

Scope

Full Site Redesign, Content Strategy, Asset Production

Role

Creative Director & Project Lead

Timeframe

6 Months

The Challenge

Crown Polymers had a product catalog that could fill a warehouse, and a website that made it nearly impossible to find any of it. Navigation was unclear, product categories weren't well explained, and the sales team had no good digital tool to point customers to.

The site was working against the brand instead of for it.

The Approach

I came in as the creative and project lead — the connective layer between Crown's internal sales team, an outside web agency, and a designer building the visual assets. I audited the existing content, rebuilt the navigation architecture from scratch, and established how a broad product line could be organized so that contractors and specifiers could actually find what they needed.

From there I directed the full asset buildout: Photoshop mockups showing product applications, studio photography of coating samples, and all copy needed to bring each product category to life. Every section had to explain not just what the product was, but where it gets used and why it works.

Information Architecture

Navigation Built for Contractors

A mega-nav organizing an expansive product line by industry and application — so specifiers can find what they need without a phone call.

The Result

A site that works the way the business actually works — organized by industry, searchable by product, and useful enough that the internal sales team uses it as a tool, not just a brochure.

Product Pages

Built for Specifiers

Individual product pages with application context, specs, and related products — giving contractors everything they need to specify without picking up the phone.
