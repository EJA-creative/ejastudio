Brand Identity / Launch

XCoatings
Brand Launch

Client

XCoatings by Polycoat USA

Scope

Brand Identity, Trade Show, Collateral, Video

The Challenge

XCoatings by Polycoat USA was a technically advanced product entering a competitive market with no brand and no presence. Everything needed to be built from scratch — identity, large-scale graphics, materials, and a full trade show environment — all cohesive enough to launch a new product with credibility.

The Work

Starting from scratch, I built the XCoatings brand identity — logo, visual language, and the design system to support it. From there: a 20×20 ft trade show booth with a 20 ft backdrop, print collateral and flyers, and video production support for a brand film shown at the show. The booth centered on a live robotics demonstration — the automation technology that makes XCoatings viable at manufacturing scale.

Every deliverable had to communicate something technically complex to an audience of engineers and manufacturers, clearly and quickly.

The Result

XCoatings debuted at CAMX with a full brand presence — identity, environment, and content built from the ground up in months.
