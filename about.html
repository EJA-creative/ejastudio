About

Creative lead embedded in the technical world.

I'm Eric Adams — an Art Director and Creative Lead with 10+ years building brand and marketing programs for technical and industrial companies. I'm based in Southern California and work with brands in chemical manufacturing, construction, healthcare, and adjacent industries.

My background sits at the intersection of creative direction and production reality. I don't just design — I manage vendors, direct shoots, coordinate trade show fabricators, brief agencies, and own the creative from concept through delivery. That full-stack approach is what makes me effective inside companies where marketing teams are lean and the work is complex.

I spent six years at Polycoat Products leading creative across 12 product divisions — building brand systems, launching websites, directing trade show programs, and producing everything from technical data sheets to 50-foot exhibit builds. Before that, I led design at Avante Health Solutions in the medical equipment space, and have worked with clients including Makita, Erin Condren, and Traffik Agency.

I started EJA Studio to work directly with technical brands that need a senior creative who understands their world — someone who can hit the ground running without a long learning curve on industry, terminology, or production scale.

B.F.A. Graphic Design — Biola University, 2012
