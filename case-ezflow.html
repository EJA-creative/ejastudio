The Challenge

EZ Flow Foam was a brand new product with no identity, no presence, and no market recognition. The parent company needed a full launch — logo, brand standards, packaging, technical collateral, and a digital footprint — built from scratch before the product hit shelves.

The product itself had strong technical performance, but the brand had to communicate that credibility immediately. In a market full of established players, a new entrant with generic packaging doesn't get a second look.

The Approach

I led the brand from naming alignment through full launch creative — starting with the logo mark and building a complete visual system from there. Every decision was made to signal reliability: clean geometry, a restrained palette, technical typography.

Packaging was designed to work at the shelf and in the field — clear product identification, application callouts, and brand consistency across SKUs. Collateral included spec sheets, sell sheets, and a landing page built to convert distributor inquiries.

Brand System

Logo & Visual Identity

Primary mark, color system, and typography standards — built to scale from packaging to digital to trade show applications.

The Result

A product line launched with a brand identity that could hold its own on the shelf and in the field.
