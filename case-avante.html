Brand / Print / Campaign

Avante Health Solutions
Brand & Marketing

Client

Avante Health Solutions (formerly Pacific Medical)

Industry

Medical Equipment

Scope

Print Advertising, Trade Show, Collateral, Rebrand

Role

Lead Graphic Designer

The Challenge

Pacific Medical had built a strong reputation servicing and selling medical equipment — but the brand hadn't kept pace with the business. When the company rebranded to Avante Health Solutions, everything visual needed to move with it: advertising, trade show presence, collateral, and internal materials.

The transition had to feel authoritative, not like a name change on old templates.

The Approach

I came in as a freelance catalog designer and expanded into the lead graphic designer role, taking ownership of all marketing channels. I produced ongoing print advertising across medical equipment categories — infusion pumps, telemetry, patient monitoring — placed in trade publications on a monthly cadence.

The centerpiece was the rebrand transition itself: designing the announcement campaign that introduced Avante to the market and retired the Pacific Medical identity across the full suite of trade show materials, sales collateral, and the ongoing ad calendar.

Environmental

Live on the Show Floor

The new Avante identity carried across the trade show booth — branded backwalls and counters introducing the rebrand to the market.

The Result

A complete marketing presence carried through a rebrand transition — consistent across every channel, built and maintained by one designer.
