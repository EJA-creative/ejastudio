The Challenge

Avante Health Solutions provides medical equipment and service solutions across a complex product portfolio — from anesthesia systems to patient monitoring and biomedical services. The scope of their offering made brand consistency a constant challenge: the same brand had to work at a trade show booth, inside a product catalog, and on a sell sheet in a hospital procurement meeting.

The goal was a cohesive brand standards system and production suite that could scale across multiple touchpoints without losing visual discipline.

The Approach

I developed brand standards that gave the internal team and external vendors a clear framework for consistent execution. From there, I applied those standards across their highest-impact materials: a multi-section product catalog, trade show exhibit graphics, and a library of marketing assets their team could deploy across channels.

Catalog production required close coordination with their product and sales teams to organize a deep SKU library into a structure that read clearly for procurement buyers — technical accuracy paired with visual hierarchy that reduced friction in the selection process.

Design

Catalog Layout & Brand Standards

Multi-section product catalog built on a modular grid — scalable across categories and print runs without redesign overhead.

The Result

A unified creative system that gave Avante's team a consistent brand voice across every market-facing touchpoint.
