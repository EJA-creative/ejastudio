The Challenge

Polycoat, Crown Polymers, ChemCo, and other divisions had always shown separately at World of Concrete — smaller booths, scattered presence, diluted impact. The new direction was to unify all of them under one umbrella brand: American Polymers Network.

The challenge was creating an APN identity from scratch that felt authoritative — while keeping each division's brand alive within it. They couldn't look absorbed. They had to look like a network.

The Approach

I led creative direction from concept through production — building the APN brand mark and visual system, then applying it across a 50×30 ft Skyline exhibit. Working from a bare frame, I developed 30+ individual graphic panels: tower walls, hero wall, meeting room graphics, hanging banners, workstations, and counter wraps.

Each division got a dedicated zone — their logos present but visually tied into the APN system. I coordinated directly with the Skyline fabrication team and delivered all final print-ready files.
