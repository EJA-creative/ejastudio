Trade Show / Brand / Web

APN — American Polymers Network
World of Concrete

Client

American Polymers Network

Industry

Chemical / Construction

Scope

Trade Show, Web, Brand

Role

Creative Lead

The Challenge

American Polymers Network needed to make a major statement at World of Concrete — one of the construction industry's largest annual events. The goal was to present their full umbrella of chemical product companies under one cohesive brand presence, communicating capability and scale to contractors, distributors, and specifiers all at once.

The Approach

I led the creative direction for a 50-foot trade show exhibit — coordinating design, fabrication vendors, and all visual production from concept through on-site execution. Every decision was made to balance brand clarity across multiple divisions while creating a physical experience that felt premium and authoritative on the show floor.

Alongside the exhibit, I designed and launched apnhome.com — a landing page that served as the digital counterpart to the booth, giving visitors a place to explore APN's full range of capabilities after the show.

The Result

A 50-foot presence at one of the country's most competitive trade show floors, backed by a digital landing page that extended the brand story beyond the event. The exhibit brought together multiple product divisions under a single, coherent visual identity.
